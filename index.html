What's Inside

Everything in one place

Six integrated pages — each powered by live data, ML inference, or cloud AI.

💬
Chat Assistant
Natural-language Q&A over Prologis financials, property data, SEC filings, and press releases. Routes questions to the right data source automatically.
🏢
Property Explorer
Filter and visualize the full property portfolio by metro area and type. Revenue bar charts, treemaps, and property cards across 20+ US markets.
📰
Press Release Intelligence
Search and browse Prologis-style announcements — acquisitions, earnings, sustainability, and partnership news with category filters and timeline charts.
📊
SEC Filings
Live financial metrics (Revenue, Net Income, Gross Profit) pulled directly from SEC EDGAR via the XBRL API. Annual and quarterly trend charts.
🤖
ML Predictions
Interactive inference from two scikit-learn models: Random Forest housing value regression and Logistic Regression subscription classification. SageMaker-ready.
📈
Portfolio Dashboard
Consolidated KPIs, revenue by metro, Revenue vs Net Income bubble chart, property-type pie, and a P&L waterfall — all from the live PostgreSQL database.
